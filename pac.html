PAC JOURNEY

HOW TO USE THE SIMULATOR

Advance the catheter through the right heart and correlate its location with the pressure waveform.

The catheter enters the venous circulation, passes through the right atrium, tricuspid valve, right ventricle and pulmonic valve, and terminates in the pulmonary artery.

Select any stage directly or use the catheter controls.

PAC COMPONENTS

Proximal infusion lumen
Infusion; terminates proximally in SVC/RA region.
RA / CVP lumen
Proximal pressure lumen for right atrial pressure.
Distal PA lumen
PAP, PAOP and mixed venous sampling.
Balloon lumen
Inflates distal balloon for flotation and wedge.
Thermistor
Present on thermodilution-capable PACs.

Fick Principle

The Fick principle relates whole-body oxygen consumption to blood flow and the difference between systemic arterial and mixed venous oxygen content.

CO = VO₂ ÷ [(CaO₂ − CvO₂) × 10]
