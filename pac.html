Fick Principle

Cardiac output can also be determined using whole-body oxygen consumption and the difference between arterial and mixed venous oxygen content.

The Fick principle relates the body's oxygen consumption to the amount of oxygen removed from blood as it passes through the systemic circulation.

CO = VO₂ ÷ (CaO₂ − CvO₂)

Arterial oxygen content (CaO₂) comes from systemic arterial blood. Mixed venous oxygen content (CvO₂) uses a true mixed venous sample obtained from the pulmonary artery.
