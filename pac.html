Fick Principle

Another method of determining cardiac output uses whole-body oxygen consumption and the difference between arterial and mixed venous oxygen content.

The Fick principle states that the amount of oxygen consumed by the body must equal the amount of oxygen removed from the blood as it passes through the systemic circulation.

CO = VO₂ ÷ (CaO₂ − CvO₂)

For this calculation, arterial oxygen content is obtained from systemic arterial blood and mixed venous oxygen content is obtained from blood sampled in the pulmonary artery.
